Before you go

Why conditions matter on the Dart

The river looks calm — right up until it isn't. A few minutes checking conditions is the difference between a good day on the water and a difficult one.

1

Wind changes everything

Even moderate winds make life uncomfortable for small boats and novice crews, particularly on the open lower estuary near Dartmouth Castle and the harbour entrance.

2

Tides run fast in the lower Dart

Spring tidal streams can reach 2–3 knots. Knowing the state of the tide helps you plan your passage and avoid fighting the current.

3

Visibility matters on busy stretches

The Dartmouth to Dittisham section carries passenger ferries, sailing craft, and hire boats alongside commercial traffic.

4

Forecasts change — check on the day

A settled morning can give way to a brisk afternoon. It's worth checking again on the day of your trip, not just the evening before.
